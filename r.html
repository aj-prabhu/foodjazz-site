Why your setup matters

I believe the way your kitchen is set up changes how you feel in it. If you have to hunt for a pan or fight a dull knife then cooking feels like a chore and DoorDash wins.

I believe small changes make the biggest difference, like a good knife and a board that stays out and the things you reach for most sitting right where your hand expects them. When your kitchen flows it feels calm and kind of fun to be in, and you end up cooking way more without trying harder.

What's in your blueprint

I write every blueprint myself from what I see on our call, so it's about your kitchen and nobody else's.

Your setup

Which tools are worth keeping out and which can go in a drawer, and where everything should live so you can get from the fridge to the board to the stove without hunting.

Your pantry

A short list built on the food you like, with what to keep stocked and how to store it so it lasts.

Your best moves

The few things that will pay off most in your kitchen, like freezing stock in small portions or which sauces are worth making instead of buying.
