Questions people ask

Do I have to take the dinner?

Not at all. If you'd rather skip it just tell me, and you can copy just your link and write your own text.

Will my friend get a hard sell?

No. I'll ask before I bring up my paid month and the blueprint is theirs either way. If the month looks like a fit I'll tell them and if it doesn't I'll tell them that too.

What if they don't live in the Bay Area?

Send them anyway. The blueprint call happens over video so it works from anywhere. I coach in person in the Bay Area and if they live further out I'll point them at whatever fits.

What if I live far away?

The dinner happens at your place in the Bay Area. If you're not in the Bay Area reach out at akshay@foodjazz.club and we'll figure out a thank you that works.

Can I send someone you already know?

Yea for sure. Anyone who signs up for the month counts.

Why ask friends?

I'd way rather meet people through friends than run ads. I end up cooking in their kitchen every week so it's nice when we already have someone in common.
