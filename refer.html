What Food Jazz is

A one month cooking intensive. I come to your friend's kitchen once a week and teach them to cook without recipes, and I build the whole month around their kitchen and what they actually like to eat and what their life looks like.

By the end they're cooking without looking anything up and they host a dinner party that makes their friends and family think they're a magician.

Questions people ask

Will my friend get a hard sell?

No. The setup plan is theirs either way. If the month looks like a fit I'll tell them and if it doesn't I'll tell them that too.

What if they don't live in the Bay Area?

Send them anyway. The setup plan happens over video so it works from anywhere. I coach in person in the Bay Area and if they live further out I'll point them at whatever fits.

What if I live far away?

Bay Area is easy. If you're further out we'll figure something out.

Can I send someone you already know?

The dinner is for people I'm not already talking to. Text me first if you're not sure.

Why ask friends?

I take four people at a time and the price goes up every round. That's the whole reason I'm asking friends instead of running ads.
